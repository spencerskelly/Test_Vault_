# tracesTo

Provisional link: the two notes are related and the relationship is not chosen yet. The translator writes it for EA connectors that fit no rule; a person, AI or Workbench writes it when unsure. Every link is a Review finding until it is replaced by a stronger relationship (W-288). It is Workbench's "new relationship" (WB-054 to WB-056).

## Direction

Written on the note that holds the field. The inverse `tracesFrom` is generated on the other note and kept out of the way (W-126).

## Where it sits

After `tags`, in the order of `relationships.yaml` (W-126). It may hold several values.

## Inverse

`tracesFrom`
