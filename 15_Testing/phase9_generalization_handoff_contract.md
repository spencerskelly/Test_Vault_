# Phase 9 local evidence handoff contract

Require a passing complete Phase 8 QEAX/ledger replay before treating a generalization orientation as review evidence. For any generalization, compare the raw source connector direction and raw start/end IDs with the corresponding ledger record. Only an exact `Source -> Destination` match may be an orientation candidate. An absent or mismatching source row remains deferred. No native certification or mutation is authorized. Keep all model data local.

This contract does not establish SysML semantic correctness and does not accept caller-supplied trust flags as authority.
