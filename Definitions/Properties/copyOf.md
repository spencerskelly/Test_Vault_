# copyOf

copyOf/hasCopy says a note is a copy of another element and lets a reader trace back to the original note. It is general, not limited to Ports (W-124).

## Direction

Written on the note that holds the field. The inverse `hasCopy` is generated on the other note and kept out of the way (W-126).

## Where it sits

After `tags`, in the order of `relationships.yaml` (W-126). It may hold several values.

## Inverse

`hasCopy`
