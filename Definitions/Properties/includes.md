# includes

includes/includedIn is membership without ownership: the whole includes a member it does not own, and a member may be included in several wholes. It is written for EA shared aggregation (W-159, amended by W-277). hasChild is ownership with one parent; hasPart is Object assembly.

## Direction

Written on the note that holds the field (the whole). The inverse `includedIn` is generated on the other note and kept out of the way (W-126).

## Where it sits

After `tags`, in the order of `relationships.yaml` (W-126), after `hasChild`. It may hold several values.

## Inverse

`includedIn`
