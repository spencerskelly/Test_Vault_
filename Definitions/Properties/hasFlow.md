# hasFlow

hasFlow/flowOf is ownership: a Port note has the Item Flow notes made from its pins; direction is carried by source/target on the Item Flow note (W-133).

## Direction

Written on the note that holds the field. The inverse `flowOf` is generated on the other note and kept out of the way (W-126).

## Where it sits

After `tags`, in the order of `relationships.yaml` (W-126). It may hold several values.

## Inverse

`flowOf`
