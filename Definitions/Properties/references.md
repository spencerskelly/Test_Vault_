# references

references/referencedBy: one note mentions another, written on the note that mentions it. First used for requirements that mention other requirements (W-167); general enough for a requirement that cites a standard clause or a document.

## Direction

Written on the note that holds the field. The inverse `referencedBy` is generated on the other note and kept out of the way (W-126).

## Where it sits

After `tags`, in the order of `relationships.yaml` (W-126). It may hold several values.

## Inverse

`referencedBy`
