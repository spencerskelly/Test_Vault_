# participants

participants is one-way, written only on the Use Case, listing the Objects, Actors, Functions and Ports that take part in it; nothing is written back on them, so their properties are not overloaded (W-172).

## Direction

One-way: listed as one-way in `relationships.yaml`; no inverse field exists.

## Where it sits

After `tags`, in the order of `relationships.yaml` (W-126). It may hold several values.
